Deployment timelines depend on your specific requirements and consultant availability. We confirm expected start dates during your free consultation call.

02
Can we influence how consultants are developed?

Yes. Before deployment, we align our development pathway to your specific technology stack, tools, and ways of working, so consultants are ready to contribute to your environment.

03
Do we choose which consultants join our team?

We assign consultants based on the capability your engagement requires. We share consultant profiles for context and welcome your input before deployment.

Very. If a consultant change is needed we handle it at no additional cost. Additional consultants are prioritised for existing clients to ensure continuity.

We act quickly. Our team will step in to resolve the issue through additional support, guidance or redeployment where needed.

Through fair compensation, structured career development and ongoing mentorship.

Every consultant is trained in Qatar, mentored throughout their placement and built for a long-term career.
