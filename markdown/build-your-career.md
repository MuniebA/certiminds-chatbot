## Got Questions? We Have Answers.

01
Is the Launchpad pathway free to join?

Not at all. CertiMinds invests in your development because we believe in your potential. Your entire pathway is fully funded from day one.

02
How long is my commitment with CertiMinds?

Consultants commit to a fixed contract following the completion of training. This gives you meaningful industry experience.

03
How long is the Launchpad pathway?

Up to 16 weeks depending on your track and specialization.

04
What happens after I complete the Launchpad?

Our focus is always on placing you into a meaningful role. While opportunities can vary, CertiMinds exists to connect capable professionals with real industry demand.

05
How are engagements assigned?

We match you to opportunities where your skills and interests align with client needs.

06
Do I need to be based in Qatar?

Yes. All placements are currently within Qatar.

07
Do I need a computer science degree?

We welcome applicants from relevant technical backgrounds. What matters most is your drive, problem-solving ability and passion for technology.

08
What support do I receive during my engagement?

You are never on your own. Our team provides regular check-ins throughout your engagement.

09
What happens when my contract ends?

Our consultants move into permanent roles, extended placements, or explore opportunities further afield. You also become part of our alumni network.

10
What makes CertiMinds different?

We do not just place you into a role. We develop you, support you throughout your engagement and invest in your long-term career.
